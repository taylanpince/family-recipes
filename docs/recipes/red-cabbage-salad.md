## Ingredients

- 1/2 small red cabbage
- 2-3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- salt

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Remove the outer leaves and the core from the red cabbage.
2. Slice the cabbage in thin strips (a sharp knife or mandoline works well).
3. Toss the strips in a bowl with olive oil, lemon juice, and a bit of salt.
4. Let it sit for a few minutes before serving — the salt softens the cabbage slightly.

## Notes

- A simple everyday salad that pairs well with rice dishes, köfte, and roasts.
- Optional: a pinch of sumak or a few chopped parsley leaves if you want a variation.
