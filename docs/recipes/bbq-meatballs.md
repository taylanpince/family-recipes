## Ingredients

- 500 g ground beef (or half beef/half lamb)
- 1 onion, finely grated
- 2 slices stale bread, soaked and squeezed (or breadcrumbs)
- 1 tsp salt
- black pepper
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 handful parsley, finely chopped

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Knead all ingredients thoroughly by hand for a few minutes until the mix is uniform and sticky.
2. Rest the mix in the fridge 30 minutes.
3. Shape into long flat köfte with wet hands.
4. Grill over hot coals, turning once, 3–4 minutes per side.
5. Serve with flatbread, grilled peppers, and çoban salatası.

## Notes

- Classic köfte köfte isi shape grills fast — don't move them too early or they'll stick.
