## Recipe details

- **Dish type:** carb
- **Cuisine:** Family
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Total time:** 25 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Tags:** rice, staple, weeknight, quick

## Ingredients

- 2 cups long-grain white rice
- 3 cups water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter (optional)

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Rinse the rice in cold water until the water runs mostly clear.
2. Bring water and salt to a boil (add oil/butter if using).
3. Stir in the rice, return to a boil, then cover and reduce to the lowest heat.
4. Cook 15 minutes without lifting the lid.
5. Turn off the heat and rest, covered, 5 more minutes. Fluff with a fork.

- Standard weeknight staple to pair with protein dishes (köfte, chickpeas, fish, etc.).
