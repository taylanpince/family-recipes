## Recipe details

- **Dish type:** salad
- **Cuisine:** Turkish
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Total time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Tags:** staple, quick, vegetarian, vegan

## Ingredients

- 1 head lettuce or mixed greens, chopped
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 small onion, thinly sliced
- 1/4 bunch parsley, chopped
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- salt

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Wash and dry the greens; chop into bite-size pieces.
2. Slice the onion thin (soak in cold water 5 min if you want it milder).
3. Toss greens, tomatoes, onion, and parsley in a large bowl.
4. Dress with olive oil, lemon juice, and salt right before serving.

## Notes

- The default nightly salad that goes next to anything — scale up or down as needed.
- Add sumak, pomegranate molasses, or pul biber to change the character.
