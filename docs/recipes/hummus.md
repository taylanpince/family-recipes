## Recipe details

- **Dish type:** side
- **Cuisine:** Turkish
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Total time:** 10 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Tags:** vegetarian, mezze, quick

## Ingredients

- 1 jar boiled chickpeas (or dried chickpeas, see Notes)
- juice of half a lemon
- 1 clove garlic (crushed)
- 1 tbsp tahini
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin
- 1–2 tbsp yoghurt (to reach the desired consistency)
- olive oil and parsley (to serve)

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Put the chickpeas in a deep bowl and mix them into a paste with the hand blender.
2. Add the lemon juice and mix.
3. Add one or two tablespoons of yoghurt to reach the desired consistency.
4. Add the salt, cumin and tahini, and mix well.
5. Adjust salt and lemon to taste.
6. Drizzle with olive oil and top with parsley before serving.

## Notes

- From dried chickpeas: leave them in water overnight, then cook in a pressure cooker.
- Serve as a mezze/dip alongside warm bread or fresh vegetables.
