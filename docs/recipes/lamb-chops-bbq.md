## Recipe details

- **Dish type:** protein
- **Cuisine:** Family
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Total time:** 30 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Tags:** bbq, lamb, cookout

## Ingredients

- 8–12 lamb chops (rib or loin)
- coarse salt
- black pepper
- dried oregano or rosemary
- olive oil
- lemon wedges (to serve)

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Season chops with salt, pepper, and oregano; rub with a little olive oil.
2. Grill over hot coals 2–3 minutes per side for pink centers, longer if preferred.
3. Rest a few minutes, finish with a squeeze of lemon.

## Notes

- A Friday-cookout staple. Pairs with roast potatoes or rice and a salad.
