## Ingredients

- 2 ribeye steaks (~300 g each, ~3 cm thick)
- coarse salt
- black pepper
- olive oil

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Take steaks out of the fridge 30–45 minutes before cooking to come to room temperature.
2. Pat dry, rub lightly with olive oil, and season generously with coarse salt and pepper.
3. Grill over hot coals (high heat) 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare, depending on thickness.
4. Rest 5–10 minutes on a warm plate before serving.

## Notes

- Great with crispy oven potatoes or potato salad and a green salad.
