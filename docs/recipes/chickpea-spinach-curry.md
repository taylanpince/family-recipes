## Recipe details

- **Dish type:** protein
- **Cuisine:** Indian-inspired
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Total time:** 35 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Tags:** vegetarian, vegan, weeknight, quick

## Ingredients

- 2 cans chickpeas (or 3 cups cooked)
- 400 g frozen spinach (or 2 bunches fresh)
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2 cm ginger, grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp paprika (or pul biber)
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 200 ml coconut milk (or yogurt to finish)
- salt
- rice to serve

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Soften onion in oil, add garlic and ginger for a minute, then the ground spices.
2. Add tomatoes, cook down 10 minutes until saucy.
3. Add chickpeas and spinach; simmer 15 minutes until thickened.
4. Season to taste; finish with a swirl of yogurt if you like. Serve over rice.

## Notes

- Balanced weeknight vegetarian main — the rice side doubles as the nightly carb.
