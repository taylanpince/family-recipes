## Recipe details

- **Dish type:** protein
- **Cuisine:** Middle Eastern
- **Difficulty:** medium
- **Total time:** 60 min
- **Servings:** 4
- **Tags:** vegetarian, vegan, weeknight

## Ingredients

- 2 cups dried chickpeas, soaked overnight (do not cook)
- 1 small onion, quartered
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 big handful parsley
- 1 handful cilantro
- 2 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp salt
- black pepper
- pinch of cardamom
- 1 tsp baking powder
- sesame seeds (optional coating)
- tahini sauce + salad + pickles to serve

<!-- GENERATED_RECIPE_METADATA_END -->

## Steps

1. Pulse chickpeas, onion, garlic, and herbs to a coarse paste — not a smooth hummus.
2. Season with cumin, salt, pepper, cardamom; stir in baking powder last.
3. Rest the mix 30 minutes in the fridge.
4. Shape into balls or patties (coat in sesame if you like) and fry at 175°C until deep golden, or bake at 220°C drizzled with oil, flipping once.
5. Serve in bowls with rice or bulgur, tahini drizzle, salad, and pickles.

## Notes

- The mix freezes well — shape and freeze raw, fry from frozen.
- Makes a great mezze platter with pita.
